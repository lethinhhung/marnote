# Marnote brand

Marnote pairs a confident editorial mark with a quiet, readable workspace. The lowercase italic serif **m** carries the identity; the wordmark stays simple and modern.

## Logo files

- `apps/desktop/public/brand/mark.svg` — primary app mark.
- `apps/desktop/public/brand/lockup.svg` — horizontal mark and wordmark.
- `apps/desktop/public/brand/mark-mono.svg` — monochrome mark for single-color contexts.
- `apps/desktop/public/favicon.svg` — browser and app tab icon.

Keep the mark square and preserve its rounded corners. The italic letter is adjusted to sit optically centered both horizontally and vertically. Leave at least one eighth of the mark width as clear space around the lockup. Do not stretch, outline, or add effects to the mark.

## Typography

- Wordmark and interface: DM Sans, weight 700 for the wordmark.
- Note titles and editorial accents: Newsreader.
- Logo letter: bold italic Georgia-style serif, embedded as text in the SVG for broad font fallback.
- Body text: DM Sans with system sans-serif fallbacks.

The logo and wordmark are display assets. Do not serialize brand fonts or colors into Markdown notes.
